Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 25, 2026

UAParanormal has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page explains our standards and what we do to prevent, detect, and respond to it in the UAParanormal app and website.

1. Our standards

  • UAParanormal is for people 18 and older. Users must confirm they are at least 18 when they create an account.
  • Our Terms of Use prohibit any content that sexualizes or endangers children. This applies to posts, photos, videos, comments, shared articles, profile information, usernames, and private messages.
  • We do not allow sexually explicit content, intimate images shared without consent, or content that exploits or endangers anyone.
  • Accounts that break these rules are suspended or permanently banned. For inappropriate or illegal content, repeated violations lead to a permanent ban, and we may ban an account immediately for serious violations.

2. How people can report concerns in the app

  • Users can report any post, comment, shared article, or private message directly inside the app, without leaving it. Reports are sent to our moderator.
  • When reporting, users choose a reason. "Inappropriate or illegal content" is treated as urgent.
  • Users can also block any account at any time to stop it from contacting them.
  • Anyone can also email support@uaparanormal.app.

3. How we respond

  • We review reports and remove content that breaks our rules.
  • When content is reported, we save a copy of it, including who posted it, so that it can be reviewed and preserved even if it is later edited or deleted.
  • When we have actual knowledge of child sexual abuse material, we remove it, restrict the account, and preserve the relevant information as the law requires.
  • We report apparent child sexual exploitation material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to other authorities as the law requires, and we cooperate with law enforcement.
  • We take the actions described in these standards in line with applicable laws.

4. Private messages

Private messages (SCIF) are not end-to-end encrypted. We do not read private messages unless a participant reports a message, when needed to investigate abuse or a security problem, or when the law requires it. When a message is reported, our moderator can see that message and a few messages before it.

5. Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety and compliance questions, including questions from regulators and law enforcement, is Michael Coomer, operator of UAParanormal: support@uaparanormal.app.
