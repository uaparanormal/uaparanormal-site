UAParanormal

A community for sharing and discussing UAP and paranormal sightings.

Android app in testing · Preparing for release

About

UAParanormal is a social app made for people who are curious about UAP, cryptids, ghosts, orbs, and the unexplained. It gives the community a dedicated place to post what they see, find others who saw something similar, and talk it through.

It is built and run by Mike Coomer, an independent developer in Kentucky. It started as a place I wanted to use myself, and I built it with safety and privacy in mind from the start.

What you can do

Share sighting reports

Post a report with photos or video, the sighting type, date, and general area.

See it on the map

Reports appear as pins on a shared map, placed by general area, not exact addresses.

Share articles and news

Link to stories and videos, add your take, and react, including a skeptical option.

Private messages

Message other members one-on-one in the SCIF.

Follow members

Follow people you trust and see their posts in one place. Verified badges mark trusted accounts.

Safety and privacy

  • Members must be 18 or older.
  • You choose how much location detail to share, and your real name stays private unless you turn it on.
  • Reporting, blocking, and moderation tools help keep the community civil.
  • You can delete your account and your content at any time.
