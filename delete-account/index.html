Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Draft for review. This page will be finalized before the app launches.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open UAParanormal and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE, enter your password, and tap Permanently Delete My Account.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, name, bio, and profile photo.
  • All of your posts, comments, and shared articles, including their photos and videos.
  • Your reactions, followers, following, and blocked users.
  • Your private SCIF conversations. They are deleted for both people.
  • Your alerts and account notices.
  • Your sign-in account and email address.

What may be kept

  • Reports that other people filed, and the saved copy of any content reported for breaking the rules, for moderation, safety, and legal reasons.
  • Backups kept by our providers for a limited time, which are deleted on their schedule.
  • Records we are legally required to keep.
  • Limited information about accounts that were suspended or banned for violations, such as a record of the violations and a protected form of the email address, which we may keep to enforce our rules and prevent evasion.

When an account can't be deleted yet

You cannot delete your account while it is suspended, or while a report about your content is still being reviewed. Try again once the suspension ends or the review is finished. Banned accounts cannot sign in, so they cannot use the in-app option.

Can't use the app?

Email support@uaparanormal.app from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request," and include your username. We will confirm that the request is yours, and we aim to process verified requests within 30 days. The same exceptions listed above apply.
